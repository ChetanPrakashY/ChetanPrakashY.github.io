These notes come out of my [summer project at TIFR](/projects/hyperbolic-encounters/) with Prof. A. Gopakumar. They collect the **1.5 post-Newtonian (1.5PN) quasi-Keplerian parametrisation** of hyperbolic two-body encounters, including leading-order **spin–orbit** coupling.

## What's inside

The notes give the hyperbolic analogue of Kepler's equation,

$$
n\,(t - t_0) = e_t \sinh u - u ,
$$

and the angular equation for the orbital phase $$\phi$$ in terms of the true anomaly $$v$$,

$$
\frac{2\pi}{\Phi}\,(\phi - \phi_0) = v + \left(f_\phi + g_\phi\right)\sin v ,
$$

where $$u$$ is the eccentric anomaly and $$\Phi/2\pi = 1 + k$$ encodes the post-Newtonian periastron advance. The radial semi-axis $$a_r$$, the eccentricities $$e_r$$, $$e_t$$ and $$e_\phi$$, the mean motion $$n$$ and the coefficients $$f_\phi$$, $$g_\phi$$ are all written in terms of the conserved energy and angular momentum, the symmetric mass ratio, and the spin combinations $$\chi_S$$ and $$\chi_A$$.
