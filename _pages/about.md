I am a fifth-year Integrated Bachelor's–Master's student in Physics at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Berhampur. I am currently in Trieste, doing my master's thesis at SISSA with Prof. Enrico Barausse.

I work mostly on **gravitational-wave astrophysics**, from the waveform models we use to interpret signals from merging black holes to what pulsar timing arrays can tell us about where in the sky a gravitational-wave source sits. I also work on **exoplanet atmospheres**, turning raw JWST observations into transmission spectra and asking what they reveal about a planet and its star.

**Current work**

- **[Waveform systematics for precessing binaries](/projects/precessing-waveforms/)** at SISSA, with E. Barausse. How much do different models of spin-precessing black-hole binaries disagree, and when does that disagreement bias what we infer?
- **[Source localization with pulsar timing arrays](/projects/pta-localization/)** with Prof. Takahashi, Kumamoto University. How well can a PTA pin down an individual gravitational-wave source on the sky?
- **[JWST spectroscopy of WASP-107b](/projects/wasp107b/)** at IIT Tirupati, with V. Khaire. A panchromatic retrieval of a warm Neptune's atmosphere that accounts for the spots and faculae on its active host star.

Before this, I did research internships at [TIFR Mumbai](/projects/hyperbolic-encounters/) on spinning hyperbolic encounters and gravitational-wave memory, at [IUCAA Pune](/projects/gw-lensing/) on lensed neutron-star mergers, and a [group project at TIFR](/projects/galaxy-clustering/) on galaxy clustering. Outside research, I've coordinated IISER Berhampur's astronomy club and tech society; see [activities](/activities/).
