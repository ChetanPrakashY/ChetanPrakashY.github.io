## Naxatra · Astronomy & Cosmology Club

**Coordinator**, June 2024 – September 2025

As coordinator of Naxatra, IISER Berhampur's astronomy and cosmology club, I organised telescope demonstrations, talks and outreach events for the campus community.

The largest event was an **X-ray astronomy data-analysis workshop** that I led. It drew **330 students from across India** and brought in invited speakers from the field.

## Science communication & outreach

- **STREAM outreach programme.** I taught school students the basics of astronomy and how telescopes work, and designed interactive activities about space science.
- **Episteme science magazine.** I wrote articles for IISER Berhampur's student science magazine and worked with the technical team that runs its website.
- **Invenio science fest.** I organised events for IISER Berhampur's annual science fest.
- **Mentoring.** I mentor undergraduates as they find their way through college life and into a field of interest.
